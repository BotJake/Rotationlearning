RS3 Rotation Board v2

Static Alt1 rotation cheat-sheet. Includes searchable categories for Melee, Ranged, Magic, Necromancy, Defence, Constitution, Utility and common weapon special attacks.

Pause tile:
- Enter ticks and click '+ II pause'.
- RS3 tick = 0.6 seconds.
- 8 ticks = 4.8 seconds (useful for Greater Barge out-of-combat stall).

Ability icons are loaded from the RuneScape Wiki while the app is online. If an exact filename differs, the tile falls back gracefully; search/category and rotation still work.

Drag rotation cards to reorder. Double-click to remove. Presets/settings save locally.
No OCR, cooldown tracking, or game-state reading.
